Find The CA for Your Desired Services

We understand that finding a Chartered Accountant (CA) is crucial to achieving your financial goals. That’s why ICAI has made it easier than ever to connect with the qualified CAs in practice available on the portal.

The platform offers you access to a network of Chartered Accountants across the country, vetted for their qualifications and experience.

Whether you’re an individual looking for personal tax advice, a business seeking audit services, or an entrepreneur in need of financial planning, you can find the right CA to meet your unique needs on our portal. All the Chartered Accountants listed on our portal are thoroughly verified. You can be confident that you’re working with a CA register with ICAI, and with the right experience.

Listing of CA firm/ Individual CA practitioner on aggregators is not permissible as per ICAI Code of Ethics since matter-based aggregators do not confirm to ICAI ethical norms. Further, other platforms/ portals may not confirm the authenticity of CA’s registration and experience, which will not yield good results for the clients.

CA Connect Portal is a system of listing CA Firms / Individual CA Practitioners on the platform of ICAI. The objective of this Portal is to provide an effective platform for listing. ICAI acts as a facilitator and enables search to the users based on the information submitted by the registered users.
